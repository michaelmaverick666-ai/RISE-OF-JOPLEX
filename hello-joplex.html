    🧬 CRISPR

    CRISPR stands for Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats.

    CRISPR is a family of biological systems that can be used to target specific DNA sequences.

    🔬 Key Components

    Cas9: An enzyme that can cut DNA.

    Guide RNA: Helps direct Cas9 toward a particular DNA sequence.

    DNA: The molecule that stores genetic information.

    🧠 Simple Example

    Think of DNA like a huge book. The guide RNA helps find the target, while Cas9 acts like molecular scissors.

    🌍 Why It Matters

    CRISPR is important in biological research, medicine, agriculture, and the study of genetic diseases.
